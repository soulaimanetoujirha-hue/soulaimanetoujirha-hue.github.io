Scintilla – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Scintilla: Ice Puzzle Story ("the game", formerly Slide Lock, package com.soulgames.slidelock) is a puzzle game for Android made by Soul87y. This policy explains what data the game handles and why.

Data the game stores on your device

Your progress (completed levels, stars, flames and best times, Sparks, hints, characters, settings and your purchases) is saved only on your phone. The game does not send this information to us or to any server we operate. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Yodo1 MAS (Yodo1 Games), which works with these ad networks: Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Liftoff (Vungle), Moloco and Meta Audience Network. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Yodo1 and these networks may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location,
  • device and app information (for example phone model, operating system, language),
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app (for example, app launches and taps),
  • diagnostic information such as app performance and crashes.

This data is encrypted in transit and handled under each company's own privacy policy: Yodo1, Google, AppLovin, Unity, Liftoff (Vungle), Moloco and Meta.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent (Google's consent form) before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off all ads except the ones you choose to watch for a hint.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

The game can show online leaderboards using Google Play Games Services. If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your star totals to Google so it can rank you against other players. Your Play Games name and profile picture are shown on the leaderboard according to your Google Play Games profile settings. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your name, email or any other account details, and you can play the game without signing in.

In-app purchases

The optional purchases ("Remove Ads" and characters) are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules at most one reminder every 12 hours, never at night. The reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

We keep no account or server data about you. Uninstalling the game deletes your progress. To limit ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or withdraw consent under Settings → Privacy options.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
