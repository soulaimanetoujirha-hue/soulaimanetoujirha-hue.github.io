Pawn to Queen privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · by Soul87y

Pawn to Queen: Chess Coach (package com.soulgames.pawntoqueen) is an offline chess app. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores

Your games, their reviews, your practice exercises, puzzle results, course and drill progress and your settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are used only to run the app: to show your progress, review your games and choose your next exercises.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send data.

  • Backups you make: "Export progress" in Settings writes a file wherever you choose to save it. It is your file; we never see it.
  • Android backup: if Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's progress in that backup and restore it on a new phone. This is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for the short buzz on moves and results. You can switch it off in Settings.

Purchases

If you buy the app or anything in it, Google Play handles the payment. We receive no card or payment details.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything it saved.
