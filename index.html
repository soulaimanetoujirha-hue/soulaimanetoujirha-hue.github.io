Scintilla: Ice Puzzle Story

Scintilla is a calm sliding puzzle with a story told in light. Swipe and Auren slides across the ice until something stops him. Reach the exit in as few moves as you can, and every perfect solve brings back a Spark: a small memory of the world before the cold.

108 puzzles across nine frozen worlds, 50 Extreme puzzles, a home world that comes back to life, and the Hearthfolk to play as. Made for one thumb.
